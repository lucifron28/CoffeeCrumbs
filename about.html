About Us

Business Description

Name: Coffee Crumb
Location: Kaybagal Center, Tagaytay City
Type: Coffee shop
Seating: 30 tables, 50 seats

Coffee Crumb

"A Cozy Haven for Affordable Coffee Delights."
Enter the warm and inviting world of Coffee Crumb, where the aroma of freshly brewed coffee and the sweetness of delectable pastries will wrap you in a cozy hug. Our objective is straightforward: to provide high-quality coffee and snacks at reasonable costs, making each day a little brighter and more enjoyable.

Our Coffee Story

We at Coffee Crumb are passionate about coffee. We acquire our beans from local roasters to guarantee that each cup is packed with flavor and affection. Our carefully created coffee drinks, which range from rich and smooth lattes to robust and adventurous cold brews, are designed to satisfy your cravings while also warming your heart.

Delicious Pastries and Food

Our pastry counter is brimming with freshly baked treats, ranging from flaky croissants and delicious muffins to savory sandwiches and nutritious salads. Whether you're craving something sweet or savory, our menu has you covered. And with our focus on price, you may enjoy your favorites without breaking the bank.

Cozy Atmosphere

Coffee Crumb is more than simply a coffee shop; it's a community gathering place for friends and neighbors to connect and exchange stories. Our cozy ambiance is intended to make you feel at home, with comfortable couches, rustic wooden tables, and vintage furnishings that will transport you to a bygone period. Whether you're studying, working, or simply enjoying a cup of coffee, our warm and inviting area is the best place to relax and unwind.

Whether you're a coffee connoisseur, a foodie, or simply looking for a warm and welcoming space to hang out, Coffee Crumb is the perfect destination. So why not swing by and experience it for yourself? We can't wait to welcome you to our cozy coffee shop!
